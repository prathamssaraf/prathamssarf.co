02 — ABOUT

I build models that see

Computer vision, deep learning and VR — built to run on real hardware.

I’m a Founding Engineer at Soxton AI in New York. I graduated from New York University with an M.S. in Computer Science (2026, GPA 3.92/4.0), where I was a Teaching Assistant for Deep Learning, a Numerical Computing tutor at Courant and a Research Assistant in AI & neuroscience. My technical paper on dichoptic foveation for VR rendering optimization is published at ACM SIGGRAPH 2026. Before NYU I earned a B.Tech in Data Science Engineering from Manipal University Jaipur with distinction (8.90/10) and the Dean’s List Excellence in Academics Award.

My research spans facial-morphing detection on the FISWG 19-feature framework with Prof. Banerjee and vision models for behavioral neuroscience with Prof. Froemke, with a focus on lightweight AI — like distilling 72B models into efficient 7B architectures. At Hewlett Packard Enterprise, ONGC and OnFees I built server-management systems that cut crashes by 30%, predictive-maintenance models that cut downtime by 25%, and real-time analytics platforms. I’m a three-time hackathon winner, including the GIDE Hackathon at a16z NYC Tech Week and the NVIDIA × Antler × Acer NYC Spark Hack, where my team deployed a real-time public-safety vision system entirely on local hardware. I work in Python, C++, Java, JavaScript and Dart, with TensorFlow, PyTorch, Hugging Face, AWS, Docker and Kubernetes.
